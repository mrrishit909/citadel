# Art direction

## Concept: architectural wayfinding under pressure

1. **Chosen - Signage-system UI.** Bold uppercase sans, Safety Lime for "go/open," Emergency Red reserved exclusively for closed/danger states, blueprint-navy translucent panels over a near-black night concourse. Reference: stadium wayfinding signage and emergency evacuation diagrams.
2. Rejected - "Security control room" (multi-monitor grid aesthetic): too literal, reads as a surveillance product rather than a wayfinding one.
3. Rejected - desaturated all-gray: failed the book's requirement that the interface itself communicate urgency; lime/red had to carry real semantic weight (open vs. closed), not just decoration.

## Palette (from the blueprint, used verbatim)
Concrete `#B6B3AA`, Blueprint `#183B66`, Safety Lime `#B9FF44`, Emergency Red `#D32F2F`, Night `#101417`, Steel `#79838B`, Human White `#F3F1EA`.

## Typography
Bold uppercase sans throughout, wide letter-spacing on labels, matching physical signage conventions; numbers in monospace for scanability.

## Component grammar
Hard rectangular panels, no rounded corners, translucent blueprint-navy fills (reading as technical drawing overlays), lime-only active states, red reserved exclusively for the closed-gate signal so it never gets diluted by overuse elsewhere in the UI.
